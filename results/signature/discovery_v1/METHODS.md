# SREBF2 candidate signature — discovery v1

Discovery only (GSE267018); no GSE271000 data were read.

Selection: primary KO-vs-WT cholesterol-depletion BH-adjusted p < 0.05, |log2FC| >= 1, concordant nonzero direction under FBS and in the exclusion-of-SRR28966285 sensitivity fit; exact-match gene annotation; exclude SREBF2. FBS and sensitivity significance are **not** required.

A negative KO-vs-WT fold change means expression decreased following SREBF2 knockout (candidate positively associated gene). A positive KO-vs-WT fold change means increased expression following knockout (candidate negatively associated gene). These are not proven direct binding targets.

Gene membership and direction are frozen here. A fully specified scoring function, ID mapping, coverage and missing-gene policy must be frozen before validation. Do not use validation DE to alter this gene list.
