# Phase 7A — CUT&Tag Dataset Discovery and Audit

## Research objective

Identify publicly available CUT&Tag datasets suitable for
epigenomic integration with SREBF2 perturbation-derived
transcriptomic signatures.

## Research questions

1. Are public SREBP2 CUT&Tag datasets available?
2. Are suitable H3K27ac or H3K4me3 CUT&Tag datasets
   available in relevant human cell lines?
3. Do these datasets include biological replicates
   and appropriate experimental controls?
4. Can regulatory signals be integrated with the
   frozen 1,326-gene SREBF2 signature?
5. Can the 181 HAP1 directional-reversal genes and
   13 OXPHOS-associated candidates be evaluated?

## Dataset inclusion criteria

- Human CUT&Tag datasets preferred.
- SREBP2, H3K27ac, or H3K4me3 target.
- Relevant cell lines, preferably HAP1, HeLa, or HepG2.
- Publicly accessible sequencing data.
- Clearly documented experimental conditions.
- Appropriate controls and biological replicates.
- Reference genome and sequencing metadata available.

## Exclusion or limitation criteria

- Assay incorrectly identified as CUT&Tag.
- Missing essential sample metadata.
- Incompatible organism or reference genome.
- Insufficient controls for the intended comparison.
- Unavailable sequencing files.

Datasets with limitations may be retained for descriptive
analysis but must not be presented as fully validated.

## Scientific interpretation

SREBP2-specific CUT&Tag may support candidate occupancy
inference following appropriate validation.

H3K27ac and H3K4me3 CUT&Tag provide chromatin-state
evidence but do not independently establish SREBP2 binding.

Existing GSE282800 ChIP-seq-derived promoter signals
remain a separate evidence layer.

## Status

Dataset discovery initiated.
No CUT&Tag sequencing analysis has been completed.
